EXIF Overlay

A privacy-first tool that turns your camera settings into clean, fully customisable overlays for your photos. Built for photographers to share their settings consistently without paid tools or locked features.

About the Project

EXIF Overlay was built to solve a common problem: sharing camera settings in a clean, consistent, and customisable way, without needing to rely on paid tools, locked features, or online processing.

Like many photographers, I’d seen others using camera settings summaries with some built into websites, some paid, and many with limited customisation. They worked, but never quite how I wanted. So I built my own.

It’s designed to be simple, fully customisable, privacy-first, usable offline, and nothing is locked behind a paywall.

Using the Tool

Missing metadata is not a problem. If an image has no embedded EXIF, all fields remain fully editable. You can enter values manually, toggle specific fields on/off, and customise the style as normal.

You can upload up to 30 images at once. The tool reads data for all of them simultaneously, allowing for consistent batch styling.

Privacy Notice

At EXIF Overlay, privacy is a must. I believe your creative data should stay where it belongs: with you.

Overview

This Privacy Notice explains how I, Adlai Dyson | Fused Frame Photography, manage information through the EXIF Overlay application at exifoverlay.com. I operate in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

My "Local-Only" Promise

EXIF Overlay is a client-side application. This means your photos never leave your device.

Photo Processing

Images are read locally by your browser. I never upload, store, or see your original creative works.

EXIF Metadata

Metadata is extracted on your CPU. No camera settings or GPS data is transmitted to my servers.

Information I Collect

To improve the tool and understand how it is used, I collect minimal, anonymous usage statistics using Umami, a privacy-focused, self-hosted analytics platform (analytics.adffp.uk).

  • Device/Browser Info: I see which browser (e.g. Chrome, Safari) and device type (e.g. Mobile, Desktop) you use to ensure compatibility.
  • Approximate Location: I process your IP address to determine your general country. This IP is anonymised and never stored in a way that identifies you.
  • Usage Metrics: I track page views and "App Open" events to see how frequently the tool is being used.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, I process anonymous analytics data based on Legitimate Interests. This allows me to maintain the website, monitor performance, and ensure the service remains functional without collecting personal data.

Data Retention

Since I do not collect personal data (names, emails, or images), there is no personal data to retain. Anonymous analytics are kept for 24 months to monitor usage trends before being aggregated.

Your Rights & Contact

Even though I don't store your personal data, you still have rights under UK law. If you have any questions regarding this notice or the way I handle information, please reach out.
